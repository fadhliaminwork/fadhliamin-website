About Me

Understanding Business. Connecting Technology.

I am a technology business development professional with an interest in enterprise solutions, business processes and digital transformation.

My work involves engaging with organisations to understand their operational challenges, exploring how enterprise technologies can address those needs, and connecting business requirements with relevant solutions.

Through my exposure to ERP, manufacturing systems and digitalisation initiatives, I have developed an appreciation of how technology supports different areas of business operations — from finance and inventory to production planning, warehouse management and shopfloor activities.

I believe successful digital transformation starts with understanding the business, not simply introducing new technology.

Projects & Knowledge

Exploring Technology in Business

Some of the technology areas and business challenges that shape my professional interests.

ERP & Modern Manufacturing

How ERP solutions help manufacturers connect purchasing, inventory, production, costing and finance.

Explore topic

When purchasing, stock, production and finance share one source of data, planners and managers can work from the same numbers. The business question is usually less about features and more about how well the system mirrors the way the company actually operates.

Beyond Traditional MRP

Exploring how modern manufacturing systems, advanced planning and scheduling, and operational visibility can support more responsive production environments.

Explore topic

Traditional material planning answers what to buy and when. Advanced planning and scheduling adds the question of what the shopfloor can realistically produce, and live visibility helps teams respond when plans change.

Shopfloor Digitalisation

Understanding how barcode scanning, QR codes, data collection and shopfloor systems improve operational visibility.

Explore topic

Capturing production events at the point of work replaces delayed paperwork with timely data. Better visibility into work-in-progress is often the first practical step towards smarter decisions.

Connected Warehouse Operations

Exploring how warehouse management systems support inventory accuracy, traceability and warehouse efficiency.

Explore topic

Scanning goods in, between locations and out creates a traceable record of inventory movement. That record supports accuracy, faster picking and clearer answers when something goes missing.

AI & Business Transformation

Exploring the relationship between business data, system integration, AI readiness and practical business applications.

Explore topic

AI depends on reliable, connected data. For many organisations, the realistic starting point is tidying up processes and integrating systems, so that later AI use cases have something solid to build on.

Digital Transformation Mindset

Why successful digitalisation begins with understanding existing processes, business priorities and operational challenges.

Explore topic

Technology works best when it is chosen for a clearly understood business problem. Listening first, mapping how work really happens, and agreeing priorities comes before selecting any tool.
